What we changed, and what's next

Missed calls are now your second largest source of enquiries at 19. In June they weren't counted at all because nobody knew they were happening. Every one now gets a WhatsApp within two minutes.

Six of the eleven ad comments came in after 20:00. If you're spending on Meta, the evening is when it works — worth telling whoever runs your ads.

Next month we're going after the 2024 recall list, roughly 90 patients. Expect more enquiries than usual in week two, and keep Thursday afternoons a little emptier than you normally would.
