What Kura does

An enquiry is not a message. It is money with a deadline.

Patients reach a practice through whichever door is nearest. Most of those doors have nobody standing behind them. Kura turns all of them into one list, and works that list until each enquiry is either in the diary or written off on purpose.

  • A number you have never had

    No practice knows how long its patients actually wait for a reply. We measure it during your free audit, then measure it every month after. That baseline is yours whether you work with us or not.

  • We undercount on purpose

    A visit is only attributed when the enquiry, the reply and the booking are all in the same thread. Walk-ins and desk bookings are excluded, so the real figure is always higher than the one we show you.

  • Someone's judgement, not a data export

    The last section is written, not generated — what changed, what it means for your diary, what we're doing about it next month. A dashboard cannot tell you to keep Thursday afternoons free.
